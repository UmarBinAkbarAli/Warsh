# Chapter 5 — Complete Content Proposal

**Status:** Proposed for product-owner review; not yet implemented  
**Chapter position:** Chapter 5  
**Working chapter title:** **Putting Arabic Together — Possession and First Actions**  
**Urdu title:** **عربی کو جوڑنا — ملکیت اور پہلا عمل**

## 1. Chapter purpose

Chapter 5 must not teach `هَٰذِهِ` and `تِلْكَ` again as isolated new words. Chapter 1 already introduced them. This chapter will make the learner **use** those demonstratives with the two structures learned afterward:

- Chapter 3: `إِضَافَة` (possession / “of” phrases)
- Chapter 4: noun–adjective agreement

It will then introduce a new possession pattern with `لِـ` and the learner’s first past-tense action word, `ذَهَبَ`.

### End-of-chapter outcome

After Chapter 5, the learner should be able to:

1. Understand and build phrases such as `هَٰذِهِ كَلِمَةٌ طَيِّبَةٌ` and `تِلْكَ آيَاتُ اللَّهِ`.
2. Distinguish `لِي، لَكَ، لَهُ، لَهَا، لَكُمْ`.
3. Understand that `لِي كِتَابٌ` naturally means “I have a book.”
4. Recognize `ذَهَبَ` as “he went.”
5. Identify the action and doer in `ذَهَبَ الطَّالِبُ`.
6. Build a basic movement sentence such as `ذَهَبَ الطَّالِبُ إِلَى الْمَسْجِدِ`.
7. Pass a 12-question chapter test covering only taught material.

# Lesson 1 — `هَٰذِهِ` with Description and Idafa

**Template:** `STANDARD`  
**Estimated time:** 8–10 minutes  
**Objective:** Apply the already-known `هَٰذِهِ` to feminine nouns, adjectives, and Idafa phrases.  
**Not taught here:** a fresh introduction to demonstratives or advanced case endings.

## Quran hook

**Al-A‘raf 7:73**  
`هَٰذِهِ نَاقَةُ اللَّهِ لَكُمْ آيَةً`

- Focus: `هَٰذِهِ نَاقَةُ اللَّهِ`
- Meaning: “This is Allah’s she-camel.”
- Purpose: combines `هَٰذِهِ` with a feminine noun and an Idafa phrase.

## Discovery cards

1. **Recall the pointer**  
   `هَٰذِهِ` = this, for a nearby feminine noun.  
   Urdu: `هَٰذِهِ` قریب موجود مؤنث اسم کے لیے “یہ” ہے۔

2. **Use it before a feminine noun**  
   `هَٰذِهِ مَدْرَسَةٌ` — This is a school.  
   Urdu: یہ ایک مدرسہ ہے۔

3. **A feminine noun may show `ة`**  
   `مَدْرَسَةٌ، شَجَرَةٌ، كَلِمَةٌ، نَاقَةٌ`  
   Reminder: `ة` is a common feminine sign, not a rule without exceptions.

4. **Add a feminine adjective**  
   `هَٰذِهِ شَجَرَةٌ كَبِيرَةٌ` — This is a big tree.  
   The adjective follows the noun and matches its feminine form.

5. **Another description**  
   `هَٰذِهِ كَلِمَةٌ طَيِّبَةٌ` — This is a good word.  
   Urdu: یہ ایک اچھی بات / اچھا لفظ ہے۔

6. **Recall Idafa**  
   `نَاقَةُ اللَّهِ` — Allah’s she-camel / the she-camel of Allah.

7. **Point to a whole Idafa phrase**  
   `هَٰذِهِ نَاقَةُ اللَّهِ` — This is Allah’s she-camel.  
   `هَٰذِهِ` points to `نَاقَةُ`, while `اللَّهِ` tells whose she-camel it is.

8. **Do not add `الـ` automatically**  
   `هَٰذِهِ مَدْرَسَةٌ` introduces an indefinite school.  
   `هَٰذِهِ الْمَدْرَسَةُ` points to a particular known school.

9. **Read the Quranic phrase in parts**  
   `هَٰذِهِ | نَاقَةُ | اللَّهِ`  
   this | she-camel | of Allah

10. **Checkpoint**  
    Use `هَٰذِهِ` with a feminine noun; then add either an adjective or an Idafa owner.

## Vocabulary retained or reinforced

`مَدْرَسَةٌ` school, `شَجَرَةٌ` tree, `كَلِمَةٌ` word, `نَاقَةٌ` she-camel, `كَبِيرَةٌ` big, `طَيِّبَةٌ` good, `آيَةٌ` sign/verse.

## Illustration

One text-free generated illustration: a nearby school, tree, and open Quran arranged close to the learner’s viewpoint. The card UI supplies the Arabic text; no generated Arabic lettering inside the image.

## Exercises

1. **Tap translation:** `هَٰذِهِ مَدْرَسَةٌ` → “This is a school.”
2. **Fill blank:** `___ شَجَرَةٌ كَبِيرَةٌ` → `هَٰذِهِ`.
3. **Build sentence:** this is a good word → `هَٰذِهِ كَلِمَةٌ طَيِّبَةٌ`.
4. **Matching:** `مَدْرَسَةٌ / شَجَرَةٌ / كَلِمَةٌ / نَاقَةٌ` with their exact English/Urdu meanings.
5. **Choose adjective:** `هَٰذِهِ شَجَرَةٌ ___` → `كَبِيرَةٌ`, not `كَبِيرٌ`.
6. **Build Idafa:** this is Allah’s she-camel → `هَٰذِهِ نَاقَةُ اللَّهِ`.
7. **True/false:** In `هَٰذِهِ نَاقَةُ اللَّهِ`, `اللَّهِ` identifies the owner. → True.

## Completion message

You did not merely recall `هَٰذِهِ`; you used it with a noun, an adjective, and an Idafa phrase.

# Lesson 2 — `تِلْكَ` in Useful Quranic Phrases

**Template:** `STANDARD`  
**Estimated time:** 8–10 minutes  
**Objective:** Apply the already-known `تِلْكَ` in far-feminine descriptions and a high-frequency Quranic phrase.

## Quran hook

**Ali ‘Imran 3:108**  
`تِلْكَ آيَاتُ اللَّهِ نَتْلُوهَا عَلَيْكَ بِالْحَقِّ`

- Focus: `تِلْكَ آيَاتُ اللَّهِ`
- Meaning: “Those are the verses/signs of Allah.”
- Scope note: `آيَات` is a non-human plural. At this level, learners memorize the Quranic phrase; the fuller non-human-plural agreement rule remains for its later dedicated lesson.

## Discovery cards

1. **Recall the pointer**  
   `تِلْكَ` = that, for a distant feminine noun.

2. **Distance matters**  
   `هَٰذِهِ شَجَرَةٌ` — this is a tree.  
   `تِلْكَ شَجَرَةٌ` — that is a tree.

3. **Use a description**  
   `تِلْكَ شَجَرَةٌ كَبِيرَةٌ` — That is a big tree.

4. **Use a known place word**  
   `تِلْكَ مَدْرَسَةٌ جَدِيدَةٌ` — That is a new school.

5. **Quranic phrase**  
   `تِلْكَ آيَاتُ اللَّهِ` — Those are the signs/verses of Allah.

6. **Read the Idafa**  
   `آيَاتُ اللَّهِ` = the signs/verses of Allah.

7. **Read the whole structure**  
   `تِلْكَ | آيَاتُ | اللَّهِ`  
   those | signs/verses | of Allah

8. **Important limited preview**  
   Non-human plurals can be treated grammatically like feminine singular nouns. Do not memorize the full rule yet; recognize this Quranic pattern.

9. **Near versus far check**  
   Nearby feminine → `هَٰذِهِ`; distant feminine → `تِلْكَ`.

10. **Checkpoint**  
    Select `تِلْكَ` from meaning, distance, and noun pattern—not by guessing.

## Vocabulary retained or reinforced

`آيَاتٌ` signs/verses, `مَدْرَسَةٌ` school, `شَجَرَةٌ` tree, `جَدِيدَةٌ` new, `كَبِيرَةٌ` big, `بِالْحَقِّ` with the truth.

## Illustration

Reuse the approved Chapter 1 distant-tree illustration where suitable. Add one new text-free Quranic visual showing an open Quran and a sequence of illuminated verse markers at a distance; Arabic remains rendered by the app.

## Exercises

1. **Tap translation:** `تِلْكَ مَدْرَسَةٌ` → “That is a school.”
2. **Fill blank:** `___ شَجَرَةٌ كَبِيرَةٌ` with a far-object hint → `تِلْكَ`.
3. **Near/far choice:** near feminine → `هَٰذِهِ`; far feminine → `تِلْكَ`.
4. **Build sentence:** that is a new school → `تِلْكَ مَدْرَسَةٌ جَدِيدَةٌ`.
5. **Build Quranic phrase:** `تِلْكَ آيَاتُ اللَّهِ`.
6. **Matching:** every Arabic item is paired with the exact meaning; `تِلْكَ` must map to “that/those,” never “this/these.”
7. **True/false:** In `تِلْكَ آيَاتُ اللَّهِ`, `آيَاتُ اللَّهِ` is an Idafa phrase. → True.

## Completion message

You can now apply `تِلْكَ` in ordinary descriptions and recognize `تِلْكَ آيَاتُ اللَّهِ` as one meaningful Quranic unit.

# Lesson 3 — `لِي` and `لَكَ`

**Template:** `STANDARD`  
**Estimated time:** 9–11 minutes  
**Objective:** Express “I have” and “you have” using `لِي` and `لَكَ`.  
**Scope boundary:** This is possession with `لِـ`; Chapter 7 will separately teach attached noun suffixes such as `كِتَابِي`.

## Quran hook

**Al-Kafirun 109:6**  
`لَكُمْ دِينُكُمْ وَلِيَ دِينِ`

- Immediate focus: recognize the `لَكُمْ` and `لِيَ` ownership/association pattern.
- The plural form `لَكُمْ` is previewed only; it is taught directly in Lesson 4.

## Discovery cards

1. **Arabic possession pattern**  
   Arabic can say “to me is a book” where natural English/Urdu says “I have a book.”

2. **`لِي`**  
   `لِي` = for me / belonging to me / I have.

3. **First complete phrase**  
   `لِي كِتَابٌ` — I have a book.  
   Urdu: میرے پاس ایک کتاب ہے۔

4. **More useful vocabulary**  
   `لِي قَلَمٌ` — I have a pen.

5. **`لَكَ`**  
   `لَكَ` = for you / belonging to you / you have, addressing one male learner.

6. **Second complete phrase**  
   `لَكَ كِتَابٌ` — You have a book.  
   Urdu: تمہارے / آپ کے پاس ایک کتاب ہے۔

7. **Contrast**  
   `لِي قَلَمٌ` — I have a pen.  
   `لَكَ قَلَمٌ` — You have a pen.

8. **Question and answer pattern**  
   `لِمَنْ هَذَا؟` is reserved for a later dedicated question lesson. Here, answer patterns only: `لِي` and `لَكَ`.

9. **Do not confuse two possession tools**  
   `كِتَابُ الطَّالِبِ` = the student’s book (Idafa).  
   `لِلطَّالِبِ كِتَابٌ` = the student has a book (`لِـ` possession).

10. **Checkpoint**  
    Speaker owns it → `لِي`; the person addressed owns it → `لَكَ`.

## Vocabulary retained or added

`كِتَابٌ` book, `قَلَمٌ` pen, `بَيْتٌ` house, `مِفْتَاحٌ` key, `دِينٌ` way/religion.

## Illustration

One text-free generated split scene: a learner holding a book on the “me” side and handing/pointing to a pen on the “you” side. The UI overlays `لِي` and `لَكَ` outside the image.

## Exercises

1. **Tap translation:** `لِي كِتَابٌ` → “I have a book.”
2. **Tap translation:** `لَكَ قَلَمٌ` → “You have a pen.”
3. **Fill blank:** “I have a key” → `___ مِفْتَاحٌ` → `لِي`.
4. **Fill blank:** “You have a house” → `___ بَيْتٌ` → `لَكَ`.
5. **Build sentence:** I have a pen → `لِي قَلَمٌ`.
6. **Matching:** `لِي / لَكَ / كِتَابٌ / مِفْتَاحٌ` with exact meanings.
7. **Choose the owner:** In `لِي كِتَابٌ`, who has the book? → the speaker / me.

## Completion message

You can now say what you have and what the person in front of you has: `لِي` and `لَكَ`.

# Lesson 4 — `لَهُ، لَهَا، لَكُمْ`

**Template:** `STANDARD`  
**Estimated time:** 10–12 minutes  
**Objective:** Extend the `لِـ` possession pattern to he, she, and you all.

## Quran hook

**Al-Baqarah 2:255**  
Focus excerpt: `لَهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ`

- Meaning: “To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth.”
- The app should display the canonical full ayah and highlight the correct `لَهُ` token.

## Discovery cards

1. **Recall**  
   `لِي` = I have; `لَكَ` = you have.

2. **`لَهُ`**  
   `لَهُ` = for him / belonging to him / he has.

3. **Use `لَهُ`**  
   `لَهُ كِتَابٌ` — He has a book.

4. **`لَهَا`**  
   `لَهَا` = for her / belonging to her / she has.

5. **Use `لَهَا`**  
   `لَهَا مَدْرَسَةٌ` — She has a school.

6. **Hear the contrast**  
   `لَهُ` ends with `ـهُ`; `لَهَا` ends with `ـهَا`.

7. **`لَكُمْ`**  
   `لَكُمْ` = for you all / belonging to you all.

8. **Use `لَكُمْ`**  
   `لَكُمْ آيَةٌ` — For you all is a sign.

9. **Quran connection**  
   `لَهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ` — to Him belongs whatever is in the heavens.

10. **Full possession table**  
    `لِي` me, `لَكَ` you, `لَهُ` him, `لَهَا` her, `لَكُمْ` you all.

11. **Checkpoint**  
    Identify the owner from the ending; do not choose from the gender of the owned object.

## Vocabulary retained or added

`سَمَاوَاتٌ` heavens, `أَرْضٌ` earth, `آيَةٌ` sign, `كِتَابٌ` book, `قَلَمٌ` pen, `مَدْرَسَةٌ` school.

## Illustration

One text-free generated possession diagram: three people with clearly separated personal objects, plus a small group panel for “you all.” Arabic labels remain native UI text, not image text.

## Exercises

1. **Tap translation:** `لَهُ كِتَابٌ` → “He has a book.”
2. **Tap translation:** `لَهَا مَدْرَسَةٌ` → “She has a school.”
3. **Fill blank:** “She has a pen” → `___ قَلَمٌ` → `لَهَا`.
4. **Fill blank:** “He has a house” → `___ بَيْتٌ` → `لَهُ`.
5. **Fill blank:** “For you all is a sign” → `___ آيَةٌ` → `لَكُمْ`.
6. **Matching:** all five forms `لِي، لَكَ، لَهُ، لَهَا، لَكُمْ` with exact owners.
7. **Build Quranic phrase:** `لَهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ`.
8. **True/false:** `لَهَا` refers to a female owner, not necessarily a feminine owned object. → True.

## Completion message

You can identify five owners through one compact Arabic pattern: `لِي، لَكَ، لَهُ، لَهَا، لَكُمْ`.

# Lesson 5 — `ذَهَبَ`: The First Past-Tense Verb

**Template:** `VERB_PATTERN`  
**Estimated time:** 9–11 minutes  
**Objective:** Recognize `ذَهَبَ` as a completed action meaning “he went” and distinguish a verb from a noun.  
**Scope boundary:** Only the masculine singular past form is actively taught. Full conjugation comes later.

## Quran hook

**Al-Anbiya 21:87**  
Focus excerpt: `إِذْ ذَهَبَ مُغَاضِبًا`

- Meaning focus: “when he went away in anger.”
- The app must use the canonical full ayah and highlight the exact `ذَهَبَ` token.

## Discovery cards

1. **Arabic now moves**  
   A verb expresses an action. Arabic calls it `فِعْلٌ`.

2. **First verb**  
   `ذَهَبَ` = he went.

3. **Completed action**  
   `ذَهَبَ` describes an action completed in the past.

4. **Do not add an unnecessary pronoun**  
   The form already carries “he”; `ذَهَبَ` can mean “he went.”

5. **Use a named doer**  
   `ذَهَبَ الطَّالِبُ` — The student went.

6. **Verb comes first in this pattern**  
   `ذَهَبَ | الطَّالِبُ` = went | the student.

7. **Another known doer**  
   `ذَهَبَ الْوَلَدُ` — The boy went.

8. **Verb versus noun**  
   `ذَهَبَ` is an action; `الطَّالِبُ` is a person.

9. **Quran recognition**  
   Find `ذَهَبَ` inside `إِذْ ذَهَبَ مُغَاضِبًا`.

10. **Checkpoint**  
    When you see `ذَهَبَ`, first recognize “he went”; do not attempt the later conjugation table yet.

## Vocabulary retained or added

`ذَهَبَ` he went, `فِعْلٌ` verb/action, `طَالِبٌ` student, `وَلَدٌ` boy, `رَجُلٌ` man, `مُغَاضِبًا` in anger/angrily (recognition only).

## Illustration

One text-free generated sequence showing a male learner leaving one location and moving away, with clear directional motion. No Arabic text in the generated image.

## Exercises

1. **Tap translation:** `ذَهَبَ` → “he went.”
2. **Word classification:** `ذَهَبَ` → verb/action.
3. **Tap translation:** `ذَهَبَ الطَّالِبُ` → “The student went.”
4. **Choose the verb:** from `ذَهَبَ / الطَّالِبُ / كِتَابٌ / مَدْرَسَةٌ` → `ذَهَبَ`.
5. **Grammar parse:** `ذَهَبَ` = VERB; `الطَّالِبُ` = SUBJECT/doer.
6. **Build sentence:** the boy went → `ذَهَبَ الْوَلَدُ`.
7. **Quran recognition:** select `ذَهَبَ` in the displayed excerpt from 21:87.

## Completion message

You have opened the door to Arabic actions: `ذَهَبَ` is a completed action, and the person after it can tell you who went.

# Lesson 6 — Action, Doer, and Destination

**Template:** `VERB_PATTERN`  
**Estimated time:** 10–12 minutes  
**Objective:** Expand `ذَهَبَ + doer` with `إِلَى + destination`.  
**New functional word:** `إِلَى` = to/toward.

## Quran hook

**Yusuf 12:15**  
Focus excerpt: `فَلَمَّا ذَهَبُوا بِهِ`

- Recognition only: `ذَهَبُوا` is a later plural form from the same `ذ-ه-ب` family.
- Active production remains `ذَهَبَ`; learners are not asked to conjugate `ذَهَبُوا`.

## Discovery cards

1. **Recall the two-part sentence**  
   `ذَهَبَ الطَّالِبُ` — The student went.

2. **Name the two jobs**  
   `ذَهَبَ` = `فِعْل` (action); `الطَّالِبُ` = `فَاعِل` (doer).

3. **Add a destination**  
   `إِلَى` = to / toward.

4. **Three-part sentence**  
   `ذَهَبَ الطَّالِبُ إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ` — The student went to the school.

5. **Read in meaning chunks**  
   `ذَهَبَ | الطَّالِبُ | إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ`  
   went | the student | to the school

6. **Another destination**  
   `ذَهَبَ الرَّجُلُ إِلَى الْمَسْجِدِ` — The man went to the mosque.

7. **A useful place word**  
   `الْبَيْتُ` = the house; after `إِلَى`, learners will see `الْبَيْتِ`. Case-ending explanation remains recognition-only.

8. **Keep the order stable for now**  
   Action → doer → `إِلَى` destination.

9. **Root-family preview**  
   `ذَهَبَ` and Quranic `ذَهَبُوا` share the going meaning. Only recognize the relationship.

10. **Meaning check**  
    `إِلَى` answers “to where?”, not “who acted?”

11. **Checkpoint**  
    Find the action, the doer, and the destination in one sentence.

## Vocabulary retained or added

`إِلَى` to/toward, `مَدْرَسَةٌ` school, `مَسْجِدٌ` mosque, `بَيْتٌ` house, `طَالِبٌ` student, `رَجُلٌ` man, `وَلَدٌ` boy.

## Illustration

One text-free generated three-stage visual: action/movement, learner as doer, and mosque/school as destination. Use visual grouping only; app-rendered labels remain outside the bitmap.

## Exercises

1. **Tap translation:** `إِلَى` → “to/toward.”
2. **Tap translation:** `ذَهَبَ الطَّالِبُ إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ`.
3. **Build sentence:** the man went to the mosque → `ذَهَبَ الرَّجُلُ إِلَى الْمَسْجِدِ`.
4. **Fill blank:** `ذَهَبَ الْوَلَدُ ___ الْبَيْتِ` → `إِلَى`.
5. **Grammar parse:** `ذَهَبَ / الطَّالِبُ / إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ` → VERB / SUBJECT / DESTINATION.
6. **Choose the doer:** in `ذَهَبَ الرَّجُلُ إِلَى الْمَسْجِدِ` → `الرَّجُلُ`.
7. **Choose the destination:** in the same sentence → `الْمَسْجِدِ`.
8. **True/false:** learners are required to produce `ذَهَبُوا` in this lesson. → False; recognition only.

## Completion message

You can now follow a complete movement: what happened, who did it, and where the person went.
