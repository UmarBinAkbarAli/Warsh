You recite it every day.
Do you understand it?

Over a billion Muslims recite the Quran in Salah. Most — including most in Pakistan, South Asia, and the global diaspora — do not understand what they are saying. They have memorized the sounds. The meaning lives behind a language barrier.

Warsh is the journey across that barrier.

What Warsh teaches

Four dimensions of Arabic mastery

Read

Classical Arabic word by word. Understand Quran, Hadith, khutbahs, and Islamic scholarship.

Understand

The grammar (Nahw) and morphology (Sarf) of Quranic Arabic — taught through the Madinah Reader.

Hear

Native pronunciation for every word and ayah. Human-recited Quranic audio — never synthesized.

Speak

Fus'ha — greetings, du'a, halaqa phrases, and confident conversations with imams and scholars.
